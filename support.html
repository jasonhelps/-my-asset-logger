Backups and sync

Check Settings → Data Health for local save, sync and backup status. Keep a full exported backup before restoring data. A daily backup and live cross-device sync are separate operations.

Subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that purchased the subscription. Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple Account subscriptions. The app evaluation does not automatically start billing.

Reporting a problem

Include the app version, device model and steps to reproduce it. Avoid emailing private portfolio records unless needed. Quotes and external news services can be unavailable; saved values remain on your device.

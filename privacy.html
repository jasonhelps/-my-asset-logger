My Asset Logger — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 6 October 2026
Developer: Jason Helps
Contact: Jason.helps@gmail.com

Your portfolio
Asset names, values, quantities, transactions, liabilities, notes, account labels and valuation history are saved in the app's storage on your device. Preferences, subscription evaluation dates and operational status are saved locally. You choose what financial information to enter. This app does not connect to your bank or execute investment trades.

iCloud
The app uses Apple's iCloud services to save portfolio state and daily backups in your private CloudKit database. Cross-device live sync can be controlled in Settings. Daily backups are a separate feature; switching off live sync does not by itself delete or disable existing daily backups. An evaluation start date is also stored in iCloud key-value storage to support the app's free period across devices. Apple processes iCloud information under its own privacy terms.

Market quotes and news
The app requests prices, ticker searches, historical data and relevant news from Yahoo Finance, Google Finance, Coinbase and Hargreaves Lansdown, depending on the features and sources you use. Requests may include ticker or coin symbols, search text, dates and currency codes. The quote/news requests do not send your portfolio quantities, purchase costs or notes. The services receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address, and may keep their own logs. Opening an article uses Apple's in-app browser; the publisher's cookies, account requirements and privacy terms apply. Prices and services may be delayed or unavailable.

Purchases
Apple handles subscription payments, offer-code redemption and purchase restoration. The app uses verified StoreKit transactions to determine access. The developer does not receive your card details through the app. Apple may provide developers with sales reports under Apple's terms. The 28-day app evaluation does not automatically purchase a subscription.

Authentication and reminders
Face ID or Touch ID is handled by Apple's authentication system. The app does not receive biometric templates. A configured PIN is stored using the device Keychain. Reminders use local notifications and their permission can be changed in Settings.

Exports and support
You can export a backup or share an asset report. Information in an exported file or email is disclosed to the destinations you choose. Suggestions are sent only when you choose to send an email, and its contents and sender details are received for responding to your request. Avoid including confidential portfolio details in support messages unless they are needed. Support correspondence may be retained while responding to your request; contact the developer to request its deletion.

Retention and deletion
Records remain until you edit, delete or restore them. The app's Delete Everything Except Backups action removes the local portfolio and attempts to delete the live cloud record; a cloud failure is reported. It deliberately preserves daily cloud backups. The app attempts to prune older daily backups as new backups succeed. Exported files, emails and other devices' local caches must be managed separately. Uninstalling the app does not itself delete iCloud records or exported files. Contact the developer if you need help managing retained copies.

Advertising and analytics
The app's source includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. This does not prevent Apple or external quote/news websites from handling information under their own policies. No custom tracking or advertising profile is implemented by the app.

Changes and questions
This policy may change as the app changes. The policy's effective date identifies the current version. For privacy or support questions, email Jason.helps@gmail.com.
